# Code of conduct

Library is made by and for people who keep their films, books, anime, and games in Obsidian. Everyone who takes part, by opening an issue, sending a pull request, answering in Discussions, or translating the interface, should be able to do it without harassment, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience, education, nationality, language, appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a second language.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Disagree with reasons, and accept that the maintainer decides what goes into the plugin.
- Give and take feedback gracefully. Own your mistakes and learn from them.
- Keep reports useful: steps, versions, and what you expected help more than frustration.
- Respect privacy. Don't post other people's personal data, keys, tokens, or private messages, and remove your own before you paste logs.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, slurs, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Harassment in public or in private, including continuing to contact someone who asked you to stop.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, advertising, and repeated off-topic posts.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a friendly, professional space.

## Where it applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, wiki, and commit comments, and anywhere you speak for the project, such as when you share it on social networks or in the Obsidian community.

## Reporting

Tell the maintainer privately through [GitHub's private report form](https://github.com/venvk/obsidian-library-plugin/security/advisories/new); start the title with "Code of conduct" so it isn't taken for a security report. Say what happened, where, and when, with links. You can also report a comment or a user to GitHub with Report content.

Reports are read in confidence. The person reporting is protected from retaliation, and nothing they share is made public without their consent.

## What happens next

The maintainer looks at what happened and its context, then may:

1. **Correct**: a private note explaining what was wrong, and possibly a request for a public apology.
2. **Warn**: a warning with consequences for continued behavior, and no contact with the people involved for a while.
3. **Ban temporarily**: no interaction with the project for a set time.
4. **Ban permanently**: for a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression towards anyone.

Comments, commits, and other contributions that break this code may be edited, hidden, or removed.
